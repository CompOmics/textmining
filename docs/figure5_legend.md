# Figure 5 legend (matches `final_analyses/MLMarkerPenalty/results/fig5_concordance_figure/figure_hamlet_vs_mlmarker_biofluids`)

**Figure 5. MLMarker confirms and refines HAMLET's project-level tissue annotation at the level of individual raw files.** (a) Agreement between HAMLET's tissue annotation and MLMarker's per-file prediction for healthy, native human runs where HAMLET names a single tissue (n = 2,775). Runs are tiered by ontology distance between the two labels: exact (same UBERON/CL term), near (subpart), distant (same organ system), conflict, or no matching MLMarker class. The last tier contains runs whose HAMLET tissue lies outside MLMarker's 34 classes and cannot be matched by construction; it includes the biofluid runs (plasma, serum, cerebrospinal fluid, urine and similar; 1,343 runs), for which MLMarker has no class at all. Dark bars, runs in which at least 10% of MLMarker's feature proteins were detected (high coverage, n = 967); light bars, runs below that threshold (n = 1,808). Percentages are within each coverage group. (b) Calibration of MLMarker's confidence (probability of the top class) on the same runs with a matchable tissue: mean Lin similarity (solid) and exact-match rate (dashed) between the two labels per confidence bin; grey bars, number of runs per bin (right axis). (c) Projects for which HAMLET lists several candidate tissues (9 projects, 473 usable files): share of files whose MLMarker top-1 prediction is one of HAMLET's candidates (top-1), whose top-1 lies outside the list but a candidate appears at rank 2–5 (top-5), or for which no candidate appears in the top five (none). Restricting to projects outside MLMarker's training set gives 59%, 33% and 8% over 133 files. (d) Distribution of Lin similarity between HAMLET's tissue and MLMarker's prediction for high-coverage runs from healthy native tissue (green, n = 823; mean 0.90) and from cancer or diseased tissue (red, n = 8,116; mean 0.38), kernel density estimates.
