Total protein and phosphoprotein profiling of mouse brain.

Phosphoproteomics of mouse brain

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Samples were prepared for total protein and phosphoprotein profiling with TMT labeling adapted from as described in PMID 29988108. Samples were cryo-pulverized and lysed with 8M Urea lysis buffer, reduced/alkylated with dithiothreitol/iodoacetamide, digested using LysC and trypsin enzymes, desalted using Sep-Pak Vav 1cc C18 cartridges, and dried in a speed vac. Peptides (90 µg per sample) were labeled with TMT10plex Label Reagent Set (Thermo Scientific 90110) according to manufacturer’s protocol.  Offline fractionation of TMT-labeled peptides was performed using an Agilent 300Extend-C18 column (4.6 mm × 250 mm, 5 µm) on an Agilent 1260 Infinity II system at 1ml/minute for 96 minutes. The 96 fractions were concatenated into 24 peptide pools and a flow through pool and acidified with s final concentration of 0.1% formic acid (FA). 5% was used for proteome profiling and 95% were used for phospho-proteome profiling.  For the phospho-proteome, the 24 peptide pools were further concatenated to make 12 pools. Phospho-peptide enrichment was carried out using the High Select™ Phosphopeptide Enrichment Kit (Thermo Scientific A52284) according to the manufacturer's protocol. The peptides were separated on an online nanoflow Easy-nLC-1200 system (Thermo Fisher Scientific) coupled to Orbitrap Exploris480 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific). 250ng of each fraction for proteome was loaded on MS, while 25% of each fraction for phosphoproteome was loaded onto the MS. The peptide loading was done on a pre-column (2 cm × 100 µm I.D.) and separated on an in-line 20 cm × 75 µm I.D. column (Reprosil-Pur Basic C18aq, Dr. Maisch GmbH, Germany) equilibrated in 0.1% FA. Peptide separation was done at a flow rate of 200nl/minute over a 110-minute run time. The MS data acquisition was carried out in TMT MS2 mode. The MS1 was done in Orbitrap (120000 resolution, scan range 375-1500m/z, 50ms Injection time) followed by MS2 in Orbitrap at 30000 resolution (HCD 38%) with TurboTMT algorithm.

DATA PROCESSING:
Raw instrument files were converted to mzML using MSConvert 67 516 . Database search for total protein profiling used mouse GENCODE (release 32). For phosphoproteomics, UniProt reviewed (SwissProt) (UP000000589) sequences downloaded on November 11, 2024.  Protein profiling:  Precursor ion AUCs and reporter ion intensities were extracted with MASIC with default parameters including butterworth smoothing method was used with a sampling frequency of 0.25 and an SIC  tolerance of 10 ppm. Reporter ion tolerance was set to 0.003 Da with reporter ion abundance correction enabled. MSFragger version 3.8 was used for database search with deisotoping and mass calibration plus parameter optimization. A strict trypsin digest rule was used to generate  peptides between 7 and 50 amino acids between 350 – 10,000 Dalton, and clipped N-terminus  methionine cleavage was enabled. Charges 2-6 were considered. Initial precursor mass error was  set to 20 ppm, and fragment mass tolerance was set to 0.02 Dalton. Isotope error values were set to  –1/0/1/2. Variable modifications were set to oxidation (15.9949) on methionine, peptide N  terminal TMT10 reagent (229.1629) peptide N terminal pyroglutamic acid formation on Q(-17.02650) or E (-18.01060), and protein N-terminal acetylation (42.010565). Fixed modifications include TMT10 reagent (229.1623) lysine and carbamidomethylation (57.02146) of cystine. Peptide validation was performed using a semi-supervised learning procedure in Percolator as implemented in MokaPot and filtered to 1% PSM FDR. Finally, peptides rolled up to the gene level with gpGrouper.    Phosphosite profiling:    Raw data were processed with FragPipe version 22.0, driven with the Philosopher Pipeline . MSFragger version 4.1 was used for database search with deisotoping and mass calibration plus parameter optimization. A strict trypsin digest rule was used to generate peptides between 7 and 50 amino acids between 200 – 5,000 Dalton. Clipped N-terminus methionine cleavage was enabled, and charges 2-6 were considered. Initial precursor and fragment mass tolerances were set to 20 ppm. The isotope error value allowance was set to 0/1/2/3. Variable  modifications are oxidation (15.9949) on methionine, phosphorylation (79.9663) of STY, and  TMT10 reagent (229.1623) on serine. Fixed modifications include TMT10 reagent (229.1623) on  N-term peptide and lysine, and carbamidomethylation of cystine (57.02146). Clear MS2 m/z range 125.5 - 131.5 to account for the TMT label. Peptide validation was performed with Percolator after addition of additional features calculated by MSBooster. Site localization probabilities were calculated with PTMProphet. MS1 peak chromatograms and MS2 reporter ions were extracted with IonQuant v 1.10.27 with default parameters for high-resolution OT-OT TMT data. Site-level  expression tables were generated with TMT integrator using the top three peptides with a min peptide probability of 0.5, min site probability of 0.75, and median normalization.
