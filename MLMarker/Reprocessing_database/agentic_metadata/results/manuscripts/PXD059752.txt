PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
instruments: Orbitrap Exploris 240
modifications: No PTMs are included in the dataset
experiment types: Bottom-up proteomics; Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Micropillar; Secretome; Modulation

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the influence of micropillars on proteomics, we seeded cells on flat and micropillar surfaces followed by (1) collection of proteins in cells on flat and micropillar surfaces; (2) collection of proteins secreted into medium by cells on flat and micropillar surfaces.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Micropillar-induced changes in cell nucleus morphology enhance bone regeneration by modulating the secretome

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Cell Lysis - Acetone wash - Insolution Digestion with trypsin - desalt - Micro BCA and LC-MS/MS Secretome - Molecular weight fractionation - depletion - Acetone Washes- Insolution washes - Desalt - MicroBCA - LC-MS/MS Group A - XW1, XW2, XW3 Group B - XW -4,5,6 Group C -XW - 7,8,9 Group D - XW - 10,11,12

DATA PROCESSING:
Raw files were convereted to mgf using raw converter and later analysed using swissport human data base in Scaffold. Label free quantification was done by searching the raw files against human database in MaxQaunt
