ABSTRACT:
The early studies showed the serine/threonine of OGG1 was phosphorylated by CDK4 in vitro, in our study, LC-MS/MS was performed, and Byonic engine was used to identify the precise site to be phosphorylated by CDK4.The present study discovered a high basal level of CDK4-mediated phosphorylation of OGG1 at S326, and this modification undergoes decline to resume a higher affinity with DNA for facilitating the role of OGG1 in gene transcription.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Assessment of CDK4 as a serine kinase phosphorylating OGG1

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Recombinant His-CDK4 was incubated with recombinant GST-OGG1 in vitro kinase assay and then subjected to SDS-PAGE and Coomassie-Blue staining  to obtain samples for machine testing. Incubate the colored protein bands overnight in a color changing solution until the blue color fades. Remove the liquid, add acetonitrile and dehydrate until the protein bands are completely white, then vacuum drain. Add 50 μ l of 10mM DTT solution to a 56 ℃ water bath and reduce for 1 hour. When the temperature drops to room temperature, add the same volume of 55mM IAA solution for light avoidance alkylation reaction for 1 hour. Overnight water bath at 37 ℃.Extract the peptide twice from 200 μ L of 50% acetonitrile and 0.5% formic acid solution for 20 minutes each, and recover the obtained peptide. When highly hydrophobic peptides are expected, perform a third extraction using 50% acetonitrile and 0.5% formic acid. Then dry and mix the extract in a vacuum concentrator. Redistribution in 20 μ L of 0.1% FA.Each sample was resuspended in buffer A (2% Acetonitrile , 0.1% Formic Acid) and centrifuged at 20000×g for 15min, 10μL supernatant was loaded onto an Acclaim PepMap C18-reversed phase column（75μm×2cm, 3μm, 100Å,  Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) and separated with reversed phase C18 column (75μm×10cm, 5μm, 300Å, Agela Technologies，China) mounted on a UltiMate3000 RSLCnano  system . Peptides were eluted using a gradient : 0 ~ 6 min 5%-8% buffer B（98% Acetonitrile, 0.1% Formic Acid）; 6 ~ 40 min 8-30% buffer B; 40 ~ 45 min 30-60% buffer B; 45 ~ 48 min 60-80% buffer B, 48-56 min 80% buffer B; 56–58min 80-5% buffer B(decreasing to 5%); 58–65min 5% Buffer B. at a flow rate of 400 nL.min-1 combined with a Q Exactive mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA). Eluent is fed directly into the Q-Exact mass spectrometer, which is set in positive ion mode and data dependency mode. Full MS scanning is performed from 350-2000m/z, with full 70000 scan resolution and MS/MS 17500 scan resolution. The minimum signal threshold for MS/MS scanning is 1E+5, and the isolation width is 2 Da. MS/MS acquisition mode is high collision energy dissociations (HCD), and standardized collision energy (NCE) is systematically checked at 28%.

DATA PROCESSING:
The MS/MS data were analyzed for protein identiﬁcation using Proteome-Discover 2.2. The local false discovery rate at PSM was 1.0% after searching against (UniProt_O15527) database with a maximum of two missed cleavages. Fixed modification: Carbamidomethyl(C); Variable modification : Oxidation（M），Gln→Pyro-Glu(N-term Q), Phosphorylation（S,T,Y）.
