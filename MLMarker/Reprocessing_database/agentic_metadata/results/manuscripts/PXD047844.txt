ABSTRACT:
CAM reagent was synthesized and used to uniquely convert cysteine residues, under mild conditions, into trypsin-recognsable sites. We apply this cysteine-capping methodology in the identification of missense variants in HCT-15 cell lines.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Cysteine capping with CAM reagent

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Cell lysate samples are treated with the CAM reagent, fractionated (with no subsequent enrinchment). Next, analyzed via LC-MS/MS proteomics methods and the output processed by Fragpipe.

DATA PROCESSING:
Raw data output files were processed with Fragpipe, and merged.

ABSTRACT (PubMed):
An electrophilic arginine mimetic, 2-chloroacetamidine (CAM), was deployed to enable trypsin-mediated proteolysis at cysteine residues and to enhance mass spectrometry-based proteomic detection of cysteine-containing peptides. Illustrating the value of the CAM-capping strategy, proteogenomic analysis using a two-stage false discovery rate (FDR) search revealed >50% enhanced coverage of missense variants, when compared to established workflows.
