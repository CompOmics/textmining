ABSTRACT:
We performed interaction and structural analyses to unravel the molecular mechanisms underlying CSN inhibition. Various methods including Cryo-EM, quantitative mass spectrometry and biochemical assays have been employed to define inhibitor-induced interaction and structural changes of the CSN complex in vitro and in cells.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Structural analysis of the CSN complex to understand its inhibition mechanism

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
HEK293 cells stably expressing HBTH-tagged CSN2 and CSN6 were treated with inhibitor or left untreated as controls and collected. Following lysis, CSN2 and CSN6 complexes were affinity purified using streptavidin-conjugated beads. Purified proteins were then reduced/alkylated and enzymatically digested using LysC/trypsin. DDA- and DIA-based LC-MS/MS acquisitions were acquired, and DIA results were used to generate a targeted peptide list for PRM quantitation of protein expression in digested lysate samples.

DATA PROCESSING:
MaxQuant (v2.0.3.0) was used for DDA-based label-free quantitation, DIA-NN (v2.0.2) was used for DIA-based quantitation, and Skyline (v.23.0.1.268) was used for quantitation of PRM data.
