ABSTRACT:
LC-MS/MS Characterization of Human Plasma Protein Corona on Polylactic Acid (PLA) Micro/Nanoplastics Before and After UV Aging.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Human plasma protein corona LC-MS/MS

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Gel sections (1 × 1 mm) were washed with 100 mM NH₄HCO₃/50% ACN at 37°C until fully destained. Disulfide bonds were reduced by incubating in 10 mM DTT at 60°C for 10 min, followed by alkylation with 100 mM IAA in the dark at 37°C for 15 min. After dehydration with ACN, the gels were air-dried and rehydrated with a trypsin solution (10 μg/mL) for overnight digestion at 37°C. Peptides were extracted using 50% ACN/0.1% TFA, pooled, and purified with a C18 SPE column. The eluates were dried, resuspended in 0.1% FA, and stored at -80°C for LC-MS/MS analysis.Nano-flow label-free LC-MS/MS analysis was performed using an Orbitrap Exploris 480 system equipped with a homemade C18 analytical column (1.9 μm, 0.15×300 mm). A 1-2 μL peptide sample was injected, with the column maintained at 60°C. The LC conditions used mobile phase A (0.1% FA in water) and mobile phase B (0.1% FA in 80% ACN), with a gradient elution at 600 nL/min. The gradient started at 5% B for 5 min, increased to 9% B, then 20% B over 20 min, followed by 34% B over 25 min, 48% B for 15 min, and finally 95% B for 10 min. Data acquisition was conducted in Full Scan/dd-MS2 mode with FAIMS set at -45V and -65V for improved sample separation. Data-dependent MS2 scans targeting the top ions were acquired using a 2 s cycle time. Full MS scans covered the m/z range of 350-1500 at a resolution of 60,000, with an AGC target of 300% and a maximum injection time (IT) of 35 ms. MS2 scans were performed with a resolution of 15,000, an AGC target of 100%, and an IT of 20 ms.
