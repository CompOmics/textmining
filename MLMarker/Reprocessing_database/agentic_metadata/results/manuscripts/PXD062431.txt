ABSTRACT:
This dataset comprises plasma proteomics data from 147 individuals measured at 60 minutes following the mixed-meal tolerance test

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Plasma proteomic profiling of the MMTT cohort at 60 minutes after the mixed-meal tolerance test

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
In the study, we employed various proteomics techniques for protein identification, quantification, and functional analysis. We began with sample preparation, including protein extraction and purification, ensuring high-quality samples for further analysis. Using mass spectrometry (MS), we applied both data-dependent acquisition (DDA) and data-independent acquisition (DIA) approaches to obtain comprehensive protein profiles.

DATA PROCESSING:
To analyze our data, we utilized bioinformatics tools and databases for protein identification, quantification, and pathway analysis. Additionally, we implemented quality control measures and validation methods to ensure the accuracy and reproducibility of our results.
