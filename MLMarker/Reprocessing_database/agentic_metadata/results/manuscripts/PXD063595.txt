ABSTRACT:
Interleukin-15 receptor (IL-15R) agonists induce anti-tumor immunity in pre-clinical models, however, dose-limiting toxicity has hampered their clinical development. An alternative is to target pathways downstream of the IL-15R to access different therapeutic profiles. We performed genome-wide CRISPR screens to reveal the complete IL15R signaling mechanism in NK cells and discovered ubiquitin-dependent IL15R degradation as the dominant mechanism restraining IL-15R signaling. Top hits included the NEDD8 E2conjugating enzyme UBE2F & ubiquitin E3-ligase ARIH2, along with Cullin-5 RING E3 Ligase (CRL5) members. UBE2F was required for CUL5 neddylation/activation whereas ARIH2 contributed to CRL5-mediated IL-15RB degradation. Ablation of ARIH2 or UBE2F enhanced IL-15RB surface expression/signaling & proinflammatory cytokine production, and augmented natural & CAR-mediated cytotoxicity. In mice lacking Arih2, Rnf7 or Ube2f, IL-15R hyperresponsive NK cells resulted in superior in vivo anti-tumor immunity against primary and disseminated metastatic tumors. Thus, we have identified the enzymes UBE2F and ARIH2 as tractable immunotherapy drug targets.

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Cells were harvested then washed three times with cold PBS and counted. For each replicate sample, washed cell pellets (1-2x106 cells) were lysed in 60mL 1X Lysis buffer (50mM Triethylammonium bicarbonate buffer (TEAB), 5% (w/v) SDS) and genomic DNA sheared using a QSonica sonicator. Protein concentration of clarified supernatants was determined by BCA (Pierce) and 20mg protein was prepared for each replicate sample in a final volume of 50mL 1X Lysis buffer To each sample, Tris(2-carboxyethyl)phosphine (TCEP) added to a final concentration of 10mM and incubated at 37°C for 45 minutes. Iodoacetamide (IAA) was then added to a final concentration of 55mM and samples incubated at 37°C for 45 minutes in the dark and then samples were acidified by addition phosphoric acid.   Samples were then diluted in 6X volume Binding buffer (100mM TEAB, pH 7.5, 90% HPLC grade Methanol), mixed and loaded onto S Trap Micro columns (Protifi). For each addition of sample (200mL maximum) columns were centrifuged at 4000xg in a benchtop microfuge and flow through buffer removed. Columns were washed with 200mL Binding buffer and centrifuged at 4000xg, 4 times and again a final time to remove trace amounts of Binding buffer before addition of 2mg of MS grade Trypsin (Pierce) in 50mM TEAB pH 7.5 (20mL). Columns were incubated overnight at 37°C and peptides eluted by step wise addition of 40mL 50mM TEAB pH 7.5, 40mL 0.2% Formic Acid and 40mL 50% Acetonitrile, 0.2% Formic Acid to the columns. Pooled eluted peptide samples were dried in a Speedy Vac and reconstituted in 30-50mL 2% Acetonitrile, 0.05% Trifluroacetic acid. Peptide concentrations were adjusted to ~0.5 mg/mL and data were collected using an Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Astral in data independent acquisition (DIA) mode.

DATA PROCESSING:
Data independent acquisition (DIA) mode tandem mass spectrometry data were analyzed using Spectronaut57 (v. 19.7.250203.62635) in directDIA mode. The four replicate samples for UBE2F knockout and AAVS1 control (total 8 samples) were analyzed independently for the NK-92 cells, donor B73 cells, and donor B74 cells, with AAVS1 controls set as the reference sample for differential abundance.
