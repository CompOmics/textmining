ABSTRACT:
To explore serum proteins associated with postoperative pulmonary infection in children with CHD, a total of 98 serum samples collected from the 23 patients before and after surgery were subjected to proteomic analysis

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Serum proteomic for pulmonary infection after pediatric congenital heart surgery

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Peripheral blood samples were collected preoperatively, intraoperatively, and on postoperative days 1, 3, and 7 and were immediately processed to isolate serum.High-abundance proteins were removed using High-Select™ Top14 Abundant Protein Depletion Resin (Thermo Scientific, Waltham, MA).

DATA PROCESSING:
Raw data were processed using Proteome Discoverer 2.4 (Thermo Scientific, Waltham, MA) against a human protein database, SwissProt (last modified on June 5, 2023).
