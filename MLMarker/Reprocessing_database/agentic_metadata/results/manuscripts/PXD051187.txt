ABSTRACT:
THP-1-derived macrophages were infected with M. bovis BCG. Separately, proteomic analysis was performed on histones (file names containing IKP) and the total proteome (file names containing JMI).

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Multi-omics analysis of BCG macrophage activation

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
THP-1-derived macrophages were exposed to BCG at an MOI of 4 for 1 hour. Cells were rinsed in warmed PBS and lysed on ice. Standard protocols for histone isolation and total proteome assessment were followed. In brief, nuclei were isolated and histones were propionylated, digested as follows, and desalted with C18 stage tip centrifugation. For the total proteome, peptides were digested as follows and desalted with C18 stage tip centrifugation. For each sample, a standard digestion protocol was followed. Briefly, 500 ug of total protein was denatured with DTT and then alkylated with IAA. Samples were pre-digested with Lys-C endopeptidase, then digested overnight with trypsin, which was quenched with 1% TFA. Peptides were loaded onto a Dionex RS3500 coupled to a Q Exactive for MS/MS analysis.

DATA PROCESSING:
The histone-focused proteome data was processed using EpiProfile version 2.1 in MATLAB R2022a. The software was configured for DDA and label-free analysis. The total proteome data was processed with Sequest in Proteome Discoverer with equivalent settings. Both searches used the Homo sapiens reference proteome.
