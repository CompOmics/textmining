keywords: Human; Dysplasia; Sessil serrated adenoma lesion; Colon

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this project is to compare the proteome of lesions of the colon, with high potentials of malignan transformation into colorectal tumor to highligh specific proteomic marker.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Comparison of Sessil Serrated Adenoma Lesion with Convential Adenoma and Healthy Colorectal Tissues

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Archive FFPE tissue slice material from seleval lesions(4µm)were treated in pooled to reach the necessary quantity for usiing FASP-FFPE. A sample is composed of the material collected from 5 patients lesions and are classified from the same group according to anatomopathological classification. Each pateint material quantity is equivalent within each pooled sample treated as one sample. FFPE-FASP kit was applied according to manufacturer recommandation as in Quesada-Calvo et al. 2017 Clinical proteomics,DOI. 10.1186/s12014-017-9143.3

DATA PROCESSING:
Raw data were treated using Maxquant v 1.5.5.1 using SwissProt database restricted to human taxonomy (20199 sequences, January 2015) and differential analysis was done using Perseus v 1.6.0.2.
