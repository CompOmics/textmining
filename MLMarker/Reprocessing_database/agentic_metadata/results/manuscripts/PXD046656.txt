ABSTRACT:
Immortalized mouse macrophages (IMMs) were stimulated with 100 ng/ml lipopolysaccharide (LPS) for 0, 30, and 60 min.  The samples were analyzed using LC-MS for global proteomics.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Global Proteomics Profiling of LPS Stimulated IMM cells

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Cells were stimulated with LPS, washed with PBS, suspended, and pelleted using centrifugation.  The cells were lysed by sonication using a Bioruptor in a buffer containing 8M urea.  The Pierce 660 nm protein quantification assay was used to measure the protein concentrations.  Each sample was reduced by adding 10 mM TCEP (final concentration) and incubation in an Eppendorf ThermoMixer for 30 min at 60 degrees Celsius.  Alkylation was performed b y adding 15 mM chloroacetamide (final concentration) and incubation in the ThermoMixer for 30 min at 37 degrees Celsius.  The samples were diluted and trypsin digested (1:25 w:w) overnight in the ThermoMixer at 37 degrees Celsius.  The samples were desalted using ZipTips and analyzed by LC-MS using a Thermo UltiMate 3000 - Orbitrap Eclipse.

DATA PROCESSING:
The resulting RAW files were analyzed using label-free quantitation using Proteome Discoverer version 3.0.0.757.  Peptides were identified using CHIMERYS.  The protein database was from UniProt (mouse; reviewed = yes, downloaded 2021-06-11).  Static modifications =  Carbamidomethyl (C).  Dynamic modifications =  Oxidation (M).  The data were filtered to require Percolator PSM FDR = 1%.
