ABSTRACT:
The protein profile of Ishikawa cells, a widely used model for uterine lining cell studies, was examined. Additionally, we investigated potential disruptions caused by liposomes containing phosphatidylcholine and phosphatidylethanolamine, serving as models for drug delivery.
