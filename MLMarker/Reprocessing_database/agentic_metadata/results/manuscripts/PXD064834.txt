ABSTRACT:
Given the pivotal role of Tat in HIV-1 latency, we performed RNA pull-down assays in latently HIV-1-infected J-Lat 10.6 cells using biotinylated RNA probes (including a control RNA probe and a Tat-specific RNA probe) . Cell lysates were incubated with streptavidin agarose resin pre-bound to Tat RNA probes, and the enriched Tat RNA-binding proteins were separated by SDS-PAGE and analyzed by liquid chromatography  mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS)

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
RBM39 organizes an m⁶A-dependent silencing complex to degrade Tat mRNA and maintain HIV-1 latency

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
RNA pull-down was performed on J-Lat 10.6 cells using High Capacity Streptavidin Agarose Resin (Thermo Scientific Pierce), according to the manufacturer’s instructions. The Tat biotinylated RNA probes were designed and synthesized by Ruibiotech. The control RNA orobes used in this study was obtained from Gong et al.[41]. Approximately 1 × 107 J-Lat 10.6 cells and 50 μL streptavidin beads were used for each sample. The biotinylated RNA probes were incubated with agarose beads at 4°C for 12 hours with rotation, after washing three time with binding buffer (0.1M phosphate, 0.15M sodium chloride). After washing the prepared cells with PBS, 500 µL of cell lysis buffer was added. The mixture was supplemented with RNasin Ribonuclease Inhibitor and a Protease Inhibitor Cocktail to prevent degradation of nucleic acids and proteins, respectively. The lysate was then incubated on ice for 30 minutes and centrifuged at 14,000 × g for 5 minutes. The the supernatants were collected and co-incubated with the RNA-bead complexes for at 4°C for 4 hours with rotation. Post-incubation, the agarose beads were pelleted by centrifugation, washed, and then resuspended with 80 μL elution buffer and 20 μL 5 × loading buffer and heated at 100°C for 10 minutes. Following the heating step, the supernatant was carefully harvested and utilized as the sample source for subsequent Western blot analysis. The samples were analyzed by 10% SDS-PAGE and visualized by Fast Silver Stain Kit (P0017s, Beyotime) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. DATA PROCESSING:
Based on the Tat-related proteomic data from J-Lat 10.6 cells, we identified 181 significantly differential proteins. The PPI network was constructed using STRING database with a minimum interaction confidence score of 0.9 [43]. In this complex PPI network, hub genes were defined as highly interconnected nodes [44]. The network was then subjected to K-means clustering (k=13), and the largest cluster containing 63 proteins involved in mRNA metabolism regulation was selected for further analysis. Network visualization was performed using Cytoscape software (version 3.10.3), and nodes were ranked using the bottleneck algorithm in the cytoHubba plugin [45].
