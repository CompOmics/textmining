PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Escherichia coli
instruments: Orbitrap Exploris 480
modifications: monohydroxylated residue; deamidated residue; iodoacetamide derivatized residue
experiment types: Bottom-up proteomics; Data-dependent acquisition
quantification: Spectrum counting
keywords: Proteomics; Oxydative stress; Bacteria; Oxydation; Methionine; Repair

ABSTRACT:
Shotgun proteomic analysis of the 15 kDa proteome of WT and ∆msrP Escherichia coli cells during chlorite stress focussed on the Spy protein

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Mass spectrometry proteomics data for the Spy protein from Escherichia coli WT and ∆msrP during chlorite stress.

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Protein samples were loaded on a SDS-PAGE, and after migration, the 15 kDa proteome was excised as a single polyacrylamide band, treated with chemical reagents for fixing the cysteine modification, digested with trypsin, and the resulting peptides were analyzed with an Exploris 480 tandem mass spectrometer.

DATA PROCESSING:
MS/MS spectra were analyzed with MASCOT, the results were then parsed, spectral counts were extracted and compared.
