PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
organism parts: Cell culture
instruments: Orbitrap Exploris 480
modifications: N-glycosylated residue
experiment types: Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Differentiation; Caco-2; Glycomics; Intestinal model

ABSTRACT:
Looking at Caco-2 cells undergoing differentiation into an epithelial-like cell model

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Glycomics of Caco-2 cell differentiation

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
cell pellets were lysed and digested with trypsin/LysC, tagged with 11plex TMT, flowthrough of an acetylation enrichment was subjected to hydrophilic interaction liquid interaction chromatography to enrich for glycopeptides

DATA PROCESSING:
data was processed using MSFragger, Percolator was run with 1% false discovery rate (FDR) filter and TMT-integrator filtered peptide spectral match and peptide with minimum probability of 0.9 for quantification.  All identified glycopeptides with N-X-S/T motif were then selected for further statistical data analysis.
