PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
instruments: LTQ Orbitrap Elite
modifications: No PTMs are included in the dataset
experiment types: Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Dna repair

ABSTRACT:
Interrogated the Ku interactome in response to DNA damage with wildtype and mutant Ku70

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Critical residues in the Ku70 von Willebrand A domain mediate Ku interaction with the LigIV-XRCC4 complex in non-homologous end-joining

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Samples were treated with IR, biotin was added and cells were left to recover for 1 hr prior to processing for MS

DATA PROCESSING:
Fragpipe was used for all data processing and downstream analysis
