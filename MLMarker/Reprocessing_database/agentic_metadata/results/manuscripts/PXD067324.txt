PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
instruments: Orbitrap Exploris 480
modifications: No PTMs are included in the dataset
experiment types: Chemical cross-linking coupled with mass spectrometry proteomics; Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Cx-ms; Mrn complex

ABSTRACT:
A chemical crosslinking-mass spectrometry (CX-MS) experiment was performed to study the structure of the human MRN complex in the presence of ATM and DNA.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Structural study of human MRE11-RAD50-NBS1 (MRN) complex by crosslinking mass spectrometry

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
The DNA-bound purified MRN complex together with ATM were crosslinked chemically using the BS3 crosslinker. Subsequently, the proteins were digested in-gel by trypsin. Peptides were measured on an Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometer.

DATA PROCESSING:
Raw data were pre-processed using the MaxQuant software (version 2.1.3.0). The extracted peak lists were searched in xiSEARCH (version 1.8.7) against a target-decoy database consisting of the protein sequences of the MRN complex and ATM. Trypsin/P specificity was assigned. Up to 4 missed cleavages were allowed. Crosslink search was based on the BS3 specificity linking K, S, T, Y residues and the protein N-terminus. Carbamidomethyl on cysteine was assigned as fixed modification. Variable modifications included methionine oxidation, BS3 mono-link with a hydrolysed or Tris-quenched end and loop link. Mass tolerance was 5 ppm at the MS1 level and 6 ppm at the MS2 level. Residue pairs-level FDR was set to 1%.
