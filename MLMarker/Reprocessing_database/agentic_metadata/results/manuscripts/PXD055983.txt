PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Saccharomyces cerevisiae (baker's yeast)
instruments: Orbitrap Astral
modifications: iodoacetamide derivatized residue
experiment types: Bottom-up proteomics; Data-dependent acquisition
quantification: MS1 intensity based label-free quantification method
keywords: New1; Lc-ms; Yeast; Differential expression

ABSTRACT:
A label-free quantitative proteomics experiment was performed to study the impact of new1 knock-out on differential protein expression in S. cerevisiae (baker’s yeast).

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Differential protein expression in new1 knock-out yeast

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Proteins were extracted from the wild-type (wt; strain: BY4741; genotype: MATa, his3∆, leu2∆, met15∆, ura3∆) and two new1 knock-out strains (KanMX; strain: new1D::KanMX; genotype: BY4741, new1∆::KanMX) (Hygromycin; strain: new1D::HphMX; genotype: BY4741, new1∆::HphMX), in three biological replicates per strain. The proteins were purified using the SP3 approach and digested by trypsin. Peptides were measured on an Orbitrap Astral mass spectrometer.

DATA PROCESSING:
Mass spectrometry raw data were processed by MaxQuant software package (version 2.1.3.0) using its default Andromeda search engine. MS/MS spectra were searched against a target-decoy database containing the forward and reverse protein sequences of UniProt Saccharomyces cerevisiae reference proteome (release 2023_05; 6,091 entries) and a default list of common contaminants. Trypsin/P specificity was chosen. False discovery rate (FDR) was set to 1% at both peptide and protein levels.
