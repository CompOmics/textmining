PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Saccharomyces cerevisiae (baker's yeast)
instruments: Orbitrap Fusion
modifications: No PTMs are included in the dataset
experiment types: Affinity purification coupled with mass spectrometry proteomics; Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Glucose signaling; Protein-protein interaction; Phosphatase; Metabolic adaptation

ABSTRACT:
PP2A-like phosphatase Ppg1 was identified as a regulator of carbon metabolism and enabled adaption to glucose depletion. To elucidate the molecular mechanism, we immunopurified Ppg1 and identified its interacting proteins by mass spectrometry based proteomics.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Identification of interacting partners of Ppg1 phosphatase

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
We introduced a FLAG epitope tag at the C-terminus of Ppg1 at its chromosomal locus and performed a FLAG affinity based purification/elution. The immunopurified fractions were resolved on SDS gels, and gel fragments were excised, The gel pieces were incubated with dithiothreitol followed by incubation with iodoacetamide. The alkylated proteins were digested with trypsin for 12hrs at 37C. Peptides were dissolved in 0.1% formic acid and were analyzed on Thermo EASY-nLC 1200nano System coupled to a Thermo Scientific Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid Mass Spectrometer.

DATA PROCESSING:
The experiment was performed wtih two biological replicates. Data analysis was performed using Mascot distiller. Searches were conducted using 10ppm peptide mass tolerance, product ion tolerance of 0.6Da resulting in 1% false discovery rate. Mascot score and emPAI values were used to compare protein abundance.
