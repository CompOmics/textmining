organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
instruments: Orbitrap Eclipse
modifications: biotinylated residue; iodoacetamide derivatized residue

ABSTRACT:
Effects to protein homeostasis processes upon treatment with cysteine-reactive covalent small molecules in viral and host proteins

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Pervasive aggregation and depletion of host and viral proteins in response to cysteine-reactive electrophilic compounds

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
HEK293T cells (often transfected with nsp14-FLAG unless otherwise noted) subjected to either vehicle or compound treatment (as stated per experiment). Samples were either taken through label-free quant bulk-proteome workflow, or subjected to an IP-MS workflow, or enriched by alkyne probe

DATA PROCESSING:
All data was searched using FragPipe version 17.0 or higher (MSFragger v3.4 or higher; philosopher v4.2.1 or higher). Label-free and probs enrichment experiments were processed through Perseus for filtering and imputation. All other downstream processing outlined in methods section.
