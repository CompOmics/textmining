ABSTRACT:
Despite improved 5-year overall survival rates in B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia (B-ALL) due to therapy escalation, effective treatments for relapsed and treatment-resistant disease, especially in specific subtypes like those with TCF3 (formerly E2A) fusions, remain scarce. TCF3, a key regulator of B-cell development, is implicated in various chromosomal translocations linked to lymphoid malignancies, such as TCF3::PBX1 fusion (5% of pediatric B-ALL) and TCF3::HLF fusion (~0.5% of pediatric B-ALL). Current omics research predominantly relies on transcriptomics, but it's increasingly recognized that this may not adequately reflect protein expression, the main targets of drugs and functional entities in biological processes. This study comprehensively analyzed proteomic landscapes of TCF3::HLF+ (n=6) and TCF3::PBX1+ (n=5) B-ALL using primary patient-derived xenografts (PDX), liquid chromatography tandem mass spectrometry, and data-dependent acquisition.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Proteomic analysis of patient-derived xenograft samples from children with B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia harboring TCF3::PBX1 and TCF3::HLF translocations

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Primary ALL cells were recovered from cryopreserved samples and injected into 8-12 week old male or female NOD.Cg-Prkdcscid Il2rgtm1Wjl/SzJ (NSG) mice as described previously (Schmitz et al., 2011). Leukemia progression was monitored by flow cytometry. ALL cells recovered from spleens of NSG mice were used for proteomic analysis. We collected 1x107 cells with >70% tumor content for protein purification. Tumor samples were lysed in a buffer containing 8 M urea and 50 mM ammonium bicarbonate (ABC). Lysates were sonicated to decrease viscosity and centrifuged at 20000 xg for 10 min. Following protein quantification by BCA assay (Sigma-Aldrich, Taufkirchen, Germany), extracted proteins were reduced by adding 5 mM DTT at 55 °C for 30 min, and then alkylated by incubation with iodoacetamide (GE Healthcare, Chicago, IL, USA, #RPN6302) at a final concentration of 10 mM for 30 min in the dark. Subseqently, samples were diluted 10-fold with 50 mM ABC to obtain a final concentration of urea <1 M before overnight digestion with Trypsin-LysC (Promega, Madison, WI, USA, #V5072) at a 1:50 enzyme:protein ratio at 37 °C. The aliquots were acidified by addition of TFA to a final concentration of 1% (v/v) for 15 min at 4 °C, and centrifuged at 2000 xg for 10 min to remove precipitate. Digested extracts were loaded onto C18 Desalting Columns (Waters, Sep-Pak Vac RC; 50 mg sorbent WAT054955) previously equilibrated with 0.1% TFA. Peptides retained onto C18 column were washed 3 times with 0.1% TFA, then with 0.1% TFA + 5% acetonitrile, before elution using 600 µl of 0.1% TFA + 40% acetonitrile.

DATA PROCESSING:
For identification, the raw files from the Orbitrap Exploris 480 were searched against the Homo sapiens (UP000005640_9606) UniProt databases, using Sequest HT through Proteome Discoverer (version 2.4).  Enzyme specificity was set to trypsin and a maximum of two miss cleavages sites were allowed. Oxidized methionine, Met-loss, Met-loss-Acetyl and N-terminal acetylation were set as variable modifications. Carbamidomethylation of cysteins were set as fixed modification. Maximum allowed mass deviation was set to 10 ppm for monoisotopic precursor ions and 0.02 Da for MS/MS peaks. The resulting files were further processed using myProMS v3.9.3 (PMID: 1761030; https://github.com/bioinfo-pf-curie/myproms). FDR calculation used Percolator (PMID: 27572102) and was set to 1% at the peptide level for the whole study. The label free quantification was performed by peptide Extracted Ion Chromatograms (XICs) computed with MassChroQ version 2.2.21 (PMID: 21751374). For protein quantification, XICs from all proteotypic peptides were used and missed cleavages were allowed. Median and scale normalization was applied on the total signal to correct XICs for each PDX. LFQ was performed following the algorithm as described (PMID: 24942700), with the minimum number of peptide ratios set to 2 and the large ratios stabilization feature.
