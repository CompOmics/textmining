ABSTRACT:
Modulation of protein-protein interactions with small molecules represents an emerging area of interest in drug discovery, enabling new therapeutic paradigms unprecedented with other drug classes such as catalytic enzyme inhibitors. Immunomodulatory drugs are a prime example for proximity-induced pharmacology where in the case of multiple myeloma, degradation of oncogenic transcription factors is achieved via recruitment of an E3 ligase complex. Here, we present a streamlined workflow for profiling small molecule degraders, integrating proteomics-based techniques such as proximity labelling, in-cell interaction validation assays, ubiquitination analysis and protein degradation monitoring. Application of this combined approach to a set of degraders enabled us to identify known and novel IMiD-dependent CRBN binders, thereby revealing the extended recruitment capacity of these compounds and raising important questions about potential degrader pharmacology beyond the dogma of recruitment, ubiquitination and degradation. This project is part of the IMI EUbOPEN project.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
ChemBioID - An integrated chemical biology workflow for the investigation of proximity-induced pharmacology of BIRC3 by the compounds AZD5582 and GDC0152

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
BirA-BIRC3 HEK293 Flp-In cell lines were grown in 10cm dishes to a confluency of 70%. The cells were induced with 2 µg/mL doxycycline and 50 µM Biotin for 24h. The cells were treated with either 1µM AZD5582, 1µM GDC0152 or 0.02% DMSO with and without 5µM MG132 for 6h. After the treatment the cells were harvested.  The treated cells were kindly provided by the laboratory of Prof. Stefan Knapp from the Goethe University of Frankfurt.  Beads were chemically modified according to Rafiee et al, 2020, in brief the beads were washed with PBS containing 0.1% Tween-20 (PBS-T) and incubated with 8.6 g/mL 1,2-cyclohexandione for 4 h on a rotary wheel. The beads were washed with PBS and incubated with 2% formaldehyde in PBS-T and 0.1 M sodium cyanoborohydride in PBS-T for 2 h on a rotary wheel. The beads were washed with 0.1 M Tris-HCl (pH 7.5), followed by 1x PBS-T. The beads were diluted with PBS-T to a 50% bead slurry.   Enrichment of biotinylated proteins for mass spectrometry analysis: Frozen HEK293 cell pellets were resuspended in 1 mL / plate (100 mm) RIPA lysis buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 8), 150 mM NaCl, 1% Triton, 1 mM EDTA, 0.1% SDS, added freshly 1 mM PMSF, 1:500 Protease Inhibitor Cocktail (P8849, Sigma) and 0.5 µL Benzonase (E1014, Sigma)). The suspension was sonicated 3x 10 sec at 35% A and 80% CT and then incubated at 10°C for 30 min. The suspension was centrifuged at 18`213x g at 4°C for 20 min. To enrich the biotinylated proteins, the lysates were incubated with crosslinked StrepTactin beads for 1 h on a 4°C on an overhead rotary wheel. The beads were transferred to a 1 µm glass fiber filter plate (Pall, 5051) and washed with RIPA buffer (1 mL, 3 times), HNN buffer (1 mL, 3 times), and 100 mM NH4CO3 (1 mL, 2 times). The Beads were transferred with 2x 200 µL 100 mM NH4CO3 to a 10 kDa cut-off filter plate (Pall, 8164). The supernatant was removed by centrifugation at 1’500x g for 15 min. The beads were resuspended in 8 M Urea and incubated at 37ºC for 15 min. Reduction of disulfide bonds was performed with 9.8 mM Tris-(2-carboxyethyl)phosphine (TCEP-HCl, 20490, Thermo Fisher Scientific) for 30 min at 37°C followed by alkylation with 18.9 mM iodoacetamide (I1149-5G, Sigma) for 30 min at 37°C in the dark. The solution was removed by centrifugation at 1’500x g for 15 min. The beads were washed twice with 250 µL NH4CO3 (100 mM). The last centrifugation was continued until no solution was left. The beads were resuspended in 203 µL NH4CO3 (100 mM) containing 1 µg Trypsin (V5113, Promega) and 0.5 µg LysC (Wako, 121-05063). The proteins were digested overnight. The peptide solution was separated from the beads by centrifugation at 1’500x g for 20 min, washed with 100 µL µL NH4CO3 (100 mM) and centrifuged again 1’500x g for 40 min. The samples were acidified with formic acid to a final concentration of 5%. The C18 plate (HNFR S18V, Nest Group) was activated with ACN and equilibrated three times with 5% ACN, 0.1% FA. The peptides were then desalted using the C18-plate. The peptides were washed three times with 5% ACN, 0.1% FA. Elution of the peptides was performed with 2x 100 µL 40% ACN (0.1% FA). The peptides were dried in a speed vacuum centrifuge. The samples were resuspended in 20 µL 2% ACN, 0.1% FA containing 0.2x iRT peptides (Biognosys). The data was acquired in data independent acquisition mode. Pooled condition samples were used to acquire library data in data dependent acquisition mode.

DATA PROCESSING:
Acquired MS/MS spectra were searched against the UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot protein database (downloaded on 04.11.2021; http:/www.uniprot.org/) using the Spectronaut software (v 17.0.221202.55965). Trypsin/P and LysC/P were selected as the digestion enzymes. The search parameters were set to allow only fully tryptic peptides with maximum four missed cleavages. Carbamidomethyl (C) was set as fixed peptide modification and biotin, GlyGly (K), Acetyl (protein N-term) and Oxidation (M) as variable modifications. The analysis type was peptide-centric. The precursor Qvalue Cutoff was 0.01. The precursor filtering was set to “Identified (Qvalue)”. No imputation or Cross-Run Normalization was done in the software.
