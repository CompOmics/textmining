SAMPLE PROCESSING:
1) SDS-PAGE: Transfer 20 μ L of beads and 5 μ L of loading buffer (5 ×) using a pipette and mix them in a new 1.5 mL EP tube. Heat in boiling water for 5 minutes; Use a pipette to take 5 μ L marker and sample into the hole of SDS-PAGE gel, then cover the top cover and connect the electrode; Set the voltage to 80 V and run for 15 minutes. Then set the voltage to 120 V and run for 60 minutes; Dyeing test: Remove the adhesive plate, peel off the adhesive and place it in Coomassie Brilliant Blue staining solution. Stain at room temperature for 30 minutes. Add distilled water and place it on a 60 rpm shaker. Change the distilled water every 20 minutes until it is completely removed. 2) Rubber strip decolorization: Cut the protein strip into 1mm3 rubber particles and transfer them into a 1.5mL EP tube; Add 1 mL of 50% ACN-50% 50 mmol/L NH4HCO3 solution to decolorize until the blue color of the gel particles disappears; Add 1 mL of 100% ACN, wait for the gel particles to turn white and form clusters, discard the ACN, and let it dry at room temperature. 3) Reductive alkylation: Accurately transfer 200 μ L of 10 mmol/L DTT solution into the sample using a pipette, reduce it in a 56 ℃ water bath for 1 hour, and then aspirate and discard; Accurately transfer 200 μ L of 20 mmol/L IAM solution using a pipette and add it to the sample. Let it react at room temperature in the dark for 1 hour, then aspirate and discard. Accurately transfer 500 μ L of decolorization solution to the sample using a pipette, vortex it, and then aspirate and discard it; Accurately transfer 1000 μ L of 100% ACN using a pipette and add it to the sample. Wait until the gel particles turn white and shrink into clumps, discard the ACN, and let it dry at room temperature. 4) Enzymatic digestion inside the gel: Add 50 μ L Trypsin enzyme (concentration: 0.025 μ g/μ L) and 50 μ L Trypsin enzyme buffer to dry the gel particles, mix well, let them fully absorb, and incubate in a 37 ℃ constant temperature incubator for 16 hours; 5) Peptide extraction: Add 200 μ L/tube extraction solution (5% TFA-50% ACN-45% water) and water bath at 37 ℃ for 1 hour; Ultrasound for 5 minutes, centrifuge for 5 minutes, transfer the extraction solution into another new EP tube, and repeat the extraction once; Combine the extraction solutions and dry them by vacuum centrifugation at 57 ℃. 6) C18 Stage Tip Desalination: Desalinate using C18 Stage Tip and vacuum dry at 45 ℃.
