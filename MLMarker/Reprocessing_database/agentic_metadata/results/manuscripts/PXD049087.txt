PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
organism parts: Cell culture
instruments: Orbitrap Exploris 480
modifications: acetylated residue
experiment types: Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Acetylomics; Tmt; Caco-2; Differentiation; Intestinal model

ABSTRACT:
Looking at the differentiation of Caco-2 cell into an intestinal epithelial model through acetylomics

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Acetylomics of Caco-2 cells Differentiating

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
cells were lysed and digested with trypsin and LysC, tagged with 11 plex TMT, then underwent immunoaffinity enrichment for lysine acetylated peptides using a PTMScan HS Acetyl-Lysine Motif [Ac-K] Kit than run on a Orbitrap Exploris 480 mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) coupled with an UltiMate 3000 RSLCnano system (Thermo Fisher Scientific)

DATA PROCESSING:
Data was processed using MaxQuant and R code MSstatsPTM
