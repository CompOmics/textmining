PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
diseases: Pancreas adenocarcinoma
instruments: Orbitrap Fusion Lumos
modifications: No PTMs are included in the dataset
experiment types: Bottom-up proteomics; Data-dependent acquisition
quantification: TMT
keywords: Dhodh

ABSTRACT:
We identify pathways of adaptation to DHODHi using an integrated MS-based quantitative temporal proteomics workflow.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
DHODHi 24 hr and 7 d proteomics

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Cell lysis and normalization, reduction and alkylation, protein precipitation, TMT labeling, trypsin/lys-c digestion, T,T Labeling, desalting, fractionation, LC/MS.

DATA PROCESSING:
Gygi lab Core at HMS was used to search data, in-house analysis completed using R Script.
