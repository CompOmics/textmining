ABSTRACT:
To identify an uncharacterized endogenous defective protein pool, we immunoprecipitated Flag-tagged TanGIBLE from HeLa cell extracts, and co-immunoprecipitated proteins werer subjected to LC-MS/MS analysis.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Identification of defective proteins trapped by TanGIBLE

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
We performed LC-MS/MS analyses using three independently prepared biological replicates. For direct immunoprecipitation of TanGIBLE-associated proteins, 4 × 106 harvested HeLa cells were washed with PBS and then lysed by sonication in IP buffer [20 mM Tris-HCl (pH 7.5), 5 mM EDTA, 150 mM NaCl, 10% Glycerol, 25 µM MG-132, 10 mM N-ethylmaleimide (NEM), 1% Nonidet P-40]. Lysates were centrifuged at 20,000 xg for 15 min at 4 °C. Protein concentration was estimated with the standard curve constructed for three different concentrations of BSA standards.  For 3xFLAG-tagged TanGIBLE or TR-TUBE immunoprecipitations, all steps were performed at 4 °C unless otherwise noted. 0.5 mL of whole cell lysates prepared from a 10-cm cell culture dish harvested 24 h posttransfection was incubated with 15 μL of anti-FLAG M2 monoclonal antibody-conjugated agarose beads (Sigma-Aldrich Cat# A2220, RRID:AB_10063035) for 1 h at 4℃. After the agarose beads were washed five times with 1.0 mL of IP buffer, bead-bound proteins were eluted for 2 h at 4℃ with 500 µg/mL 3xFLAG-peptide (F4799, SIGMA-Aldrich) buffered in PBS containing 25 µM MG-132, 10 mM NEM. Proteins were reduced in 10 mM dithiothreitol (DTT) for 10 min at 70 °C and then alkylated with 15 mM iodoacetamide (IAA) for 15 min at room temperature for on-beads tryptic digestion based on a single-pot solid-phase-enhanced sample preparation (SP3) method using a KingFisher APEX (Thermo Fisher Scientific)(Müller et al., 2020). Alkylated proteins were incubated in 200 μL of 50% EtOH and conjugated to 40 µg of an equal mixture of hydrophobic and hydrophilic SeraMag SpeedBead carboxylate-modified magnetic particles (Cytiva), followed by desalting thrice with 400 μL of 80% EtOH. The magnetic beads were subjected to enzymatic digestion in 100 μL of 5 ng/μL Trypsin Gold (Promega) in 50 mM triethylammonium bicarbonate (TEAB) for 3.5 h at 37°C and rinsed out with 100 μL of pure water. overnight at 37 °C with 1 μg of trypsin (Promega). Digested peptides were acidified with 20 μL of 0.1% TFA and loaded into Evotip Pure (Evosep). Desalted tryptic digests were analyzed by Evosep One (Evosep) coupled to an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos mass spectrometer (Thermo Scientific). Peptides were separated on a C18 analytical column [Aurora ELITE (1.7 μm, 75-μm i.d., and 15-cm length); IonOpticks] using the defined 40SPD program. The Orbitrap Fusion Lumos mass spectrometer was operated in the data-dependent MS/MS mode with a mass range of 350–1300 m/z and resolution of 120,000 using Xcalibur software (Thermo Xcalibur, RRID:SCR_014593). The most intense ions (cycle time 3 s) were selected for MS/MS fragmentation with the HCD collision energy of 30, isolation window at 1.6 m/z and maximum injection time at 22 ms in the centroid mode.

DATA PROCESSING:
Proteome Discoverer software (version 3.1; Thermo Scientific, RRID:SCR_014477) was used to generate peak lists. The MS/MS spectra were searched against a UniProt Knowledgebase (Homo sapiens sp_canonical (TaxID=9609) version 2023-09-13) using the Sequest HT search engine. The precursor and fragment mass tolerances were set to 10 ppm and 0.02 Da, respectively. Methionine oxidation, protein amino-terminal acetylation, and Gln/Asn deamidation were set as variable modifications, and Cys carbamidomethylation modification was set as a static modification for database searching. Peptide identification was filtered at a 1% false discovery rate. To identify specific targets of TanGIBLE, the results of three individual samples were assembled into one multi-consensus report using Proteome Discoverer software.
