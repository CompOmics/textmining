ABSTRACT:
No Treg-specific therapeutics has been approved for clinical use. Our project aim to screen the Treg inhibitor and investigate its mechanism in tumor.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
to screen the Treg inhibitor and investigate its mechanism in tumor

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
293T-RUNX1-Clu-Myc cells (3×107) were pretreated with MG132 (5 μM) for 1 hour, followed by treatment with Lac for 6 hours. Subsequently, immunoprecipitants with RUNX1 antibody or IgG were subjected to IP-MS analysis in three biological replicates. Silver staining was performed according to rapid silver staining kit's protocol. The strip was divided into several components (strip width is about 2mm) followed by in-gel digestion (zhou et al., 2021). The product of in-gel digestion was applied to the mass spectrometry assays.

DATA PROCESSING:
The raw data were processed with Proteome Discoverer (PD, version 2.4). MS/MS spectra were searched against the UniProt human proteome database.
