ABSTRACT:
To further elucidate the interaction between RBM39 and its partern proteins, Flag-tagged RBM39 were transfected into HEK293T cells. After transfection, RBM39 and RBM39-bound proteins were enriched using anti-Flag beads. Subsequently, the enriched proteins were separated by SDS-PAGE and analyzed by LC-MS/MS.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
RBM39 organizes an m⁶A-dependent silencing complex to degrade Tat mRNA and maintain HIV-1 latency

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Flag-tagged RBM39 were transfected into HEK293T cells. 48 hours post transfection, cells were harvested and IP with anti-Flag beads. Enriched proteins were washed and eluted by boiling with 5 × protein SDS-PAGE loading buffer. Proteins of each sample were separated by 4-12% gradient SDS-PAGE gel. Eight slices were cut out for each sample. The in-gel digestion and LC-MS/MS analysis were the same as GFP-Flag control samples.

DATA PROCESSING:
Flag-tagged RBM39 and associated proteins enriched by anti-Flag immunoprecipitation were separated by SDS-PAGE and analyzed by LC-MS/MS using an Easy-nLC 1000 system coupled to a Thermo Q Exactive mass spectrometer operated with Thermo Q Exactive operational software and Thermo Xcalibur Sequence setup. Gene Ontology enrichment analysis was performed on the identified proteins to determine significantly enriched biological pathways. The Sankey diagram was constructed to visualize the connections between identified proteins and their associated RNA processing pathways, revealing the functional network of RBM39-interacting proteins.
