ABSTRACT:
The interactome to H3 peptides in different H3K4 methylation states and its change upon H3Q5 serotonylation was determined by performing peptide pull-down followed by qMS. Streptaviding beads, saturated with biotinylated H3 peptides in all possible H3K4 methylation states and with presence and absence of H3Q5 serotonylation.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Histone peptide pull-down H3K4me0-3 +/- H3Q5ser

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
HeLa nuclear extract was prepared using a modified version of the Dignam procedure. Biotinylated H3 (1-13) peptides immobilized on Dynabeads™ M-280 Streptavidin (Invitrogen) were incubated on a rotating wheel with 1 mg of HeLa nuclear extract overnight at 4°C in binding buffer (20 mM HEPES-KOH pH 7.9, 300 mM NaCl, 20% glycerol, 2 mM MgCl2, 0.2 mM EDTA, 0.1% NP40, 0.5 mM DTT and 1x cOmplete™ Protease Inhibitor Cocktail (Roche)).  The beads were then washed two times with binding buffer containing 0.5% NP40, two times with phosphate buffered saline (PBS) containing 0.5% NP40 and two times with plain PBS. On-bead digestion of bound proteins was performed overnight at 22°C with 0.1 g/ml of trypsin in elution buffer (100 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.5, 2 M urea, 10 mM DTT). The eluted peptides were then bound to C18 stage tips (ThermoFischer), washed three times in 0.1% trifluoroacetic acid (TFA) and eluted from the C18 stage tips in 65% acetonitrile and dried prior to resuspension in 10% formic acid.  A third of the final elution was analysed. For nanoflow-HPLC-MS/MS samples were analysed on an Orbitrap Fusion Lumos mass spectrometer coupled to an Easy nano-LC 1200 HPLC (Thermo Fisher Scientific). Peptide separation was performed with a C18 separation column of 25 cm length (75 µm i.d., 2 µm particle size, 100 Ǻ pore size) with a gradient of two buffers with increasing organic proportion (buffer A: 0.1 % formic acid, buffer B: 0.1 % formic acid in 80 % acetonitrile). The flow rate was 300 nl/min. For tandem MS a TOP-10 Method was used. Each MS scan was followed by a maximum of 10 MS/MS scans in the data-dependent mode.

DATA PROCESSING:
The raw data files were analyzed with MaxQuant software (version 1.6.17.0) using Uniprot human FASTA database. Label-free quantification values (LFQ) and match between run options were selected. Intensity based absolute quantification (iBAQ) algorithm was also activated for subsequent relative protein abundance estimation. The obtained protein files were analyzed by Perseus software (version 1.6.0.7) in which contaminants and reverse hits were filtered out. Protein identification based on non-unique peptides as well as proteins identified by only one peptide in the different triplicates were excluded to increase protein predictionaccuracy.
