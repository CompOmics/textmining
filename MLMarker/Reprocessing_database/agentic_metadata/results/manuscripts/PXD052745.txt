ABSTRACT:
We present a comprehensive proteomic dataset of binding proteins for all 17 members of the poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) family, generated using the TurboID proximity labeling technique in HEK293T cells under controlled conditions. This dataset encompasses 6,325 proteins that interact with PARPs, providing valuable insights into the collaborative roles of PARP family members in cellular processes. The data include detailed protein interaction information and are available in a publicly accessible repository, facilitating reuse and further analysis by the research community.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Comprehensive Dataset of Interactors for the Entire PARP Family Using TurboID Proximity Labeling

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
In the 293T cell line we performed TurbID assay separately on PARP1 ~ PARP4, PARP5A, PARP5B, PARP6 ~ PARP16 (totally 17 PARPs) to access its potential binding protein in a high-through way. Peptidomic profiling utilized LC-MS/MS, combining Easy nLC 1200 and Orbitrap Fusion mass spectrometer.The process began with the separation of peptides using a capillary column (75 μm × 150 mm) filled with C18 AQ stationary phase with a 1.9 μm particle size.Tryptic digests, each at a precisely measured 500 ng for mass spectrometry analysis, followed a gradient of 1-5 min, 5% to 8% ACN; 5-90 min, 8% to 40% ACN; and 90-110 min, 40% to 100% ACN.

DATA PROCESSING:
Data acquisition was performed on a Fusion mass spectrometer in positive mode, with all MS and MS/MS spectra captured in data-dependent mode, initiating with an MS full scan from 300 to 1800 m/z, followed by 10 MS/MS scans. The analysis utilized Proteome Discoverer (PD) version 2.4.1 for processing raw data files, with searches against UniProt Homo sapiens reference proteome (Proteome ID: UP000005640), containing 82,485 entries from 20,597 genes. Search parameters included trypsin digestion allowing up to two missed cleavages, carbamidomethylation of cysteine as a fixed modification, and variable modifications of oxidation (M), acetylation at protein N-termini, and biotinylation on lysine. Precursor mass tolerance was set at 10 ppm and the fragment mass tolerance was set to 0.6 Da, and the FDR threshold was maintained at less than 1% for all identified peptides and proteins, emphasizing the rigorous standards applied throughout the study to ensure the accuracy of peptide identifications.
