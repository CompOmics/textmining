ABSTRACT:
Modulation of protein-protein interactions with small molecules represents an emerging area of interest in drug discovery. Here we probe the effects of catalytic inhibition on the proximity of CAMKK2. The experiment was supervised by Sabrina Keller and Viviane Reber. This project is part of the IMI EUbOPEN project.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
ChemBioID - An integrated chemical biology workflow for the investigation of proximity-induced pharmacology

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Ct-miniTurboID-CAMKK2 HEK293 Flp-In cell lines were grown in DMEM with 10% dialyzed FBS (BioConcept) and 99.0 U/mL Penicillin-Streptomycin (15140-122, GIBCO). Prior to labelling, doxycycline (Sigma) was added to the cell culture media to a concentration of 1.3 μg/mL for 24 h. For miniTurboID based biotin labelling, 10 μM SGC-CAMKK2-1, 10 μM SGC-CAMKK2-1N or 0.1% DMSO was added and incubated for 3 h, then 50 μM Biotin (Sigma) was added to the cells for 1 h. The cells were detached with 10 mL ice-cold PBS containing 1 mM EDTA. The cells were centrifuged at 300x g for 4 min, washed with 1 mL of ice-cold PBS containing 1 mM EDTA. After removing the supernatant, the cells were flash-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80°C. Beads were chemically modified according to Rafiee et al, 2020, in brief the beads were washed with PBS containing 0.1% Tween-20 (PBS-T) and incubated with 8.6 g/mL 1,2-cyclohexandione for 4 h on a rotary wheel. The beads were washed with PBS and incubated with 2% formaldehyde in PBS-T and 0.1 M sodium cyanoborohydride in PBS-T for 2 h on a rotary wheel. The beads were washed with 0.1 M Tris-HCl (pH 7.5), followed by 1x PBS-T. The beads were diluted with PBS-T to a 50% bead slurry.   Enrichment of biotinylated proteins for mass spectrometry analysis: Frozen HEK293 cell pellets were resuspended in 1 mL / plate (150 mm) RIPA lysis buffer (50 mM Tris-HCl (pH 8), 150 mM NaCl, 1% Triton, 1 mM EDTA, 0.1% SDS, added freshly 1 mM PMSF, 1:500 Protease Inhibitor Cocktail (P8849, Sigma) and 0.5 µL Benzonase (E1014, Sigma)). The suspension was sonicated 3x 10 sec at 35% A and 80% CT and then incubated at 10°C for 30 min. The suspension was centrifuged at 18`213x g at 4°C for 20 min. To enrich the biotinylated proteins, the lysates were incubated with crosslinked StrepTactin beads for 1 h on a 4°C on an overhead rotary wheel. The beads were transferred to a 1 µm glass fiber filter plate (Pall, 5051) and washed with RIPA buffer (1 mL, 3 times), HNN buffer (1 mL, 3 times), and 100 mM NH4CO3 (1 mL, 2 times). The Beads were transferred with 2x 200 µL 100 mM NH4CO3 to a 10 kDa cut-off filter plate (Pall, 8164). The supernatant was removed by centrifugation at 1’500x g for 15 min. The beads were resuspended in 8 M Urea and incubated at 37ºC for 15 min. Reduction of disulfide bonds was performed with 9.8 mM Tris-(2-carboxyethyl)phosphine (TCEP-HCl, 20490, Thermo Fisher Scientific) for 30 min at 37°C followed by alkylation with 18.9 mM iodoacetamide (I1149-5G, Sigma) for 30 min at 37°C in the dark. The solution was removed by centrifugation at 1’500x g for 15 min. The beads were washed twice with 250 µL NH4CO3 (100 mM). The last centrifugation was continued until no solution was left. The beads were resuspended in 203 µL NH4CO3 (100 mM) containing 1 µg Trypsin (V5113, Promega) and 0.5 µg LysC (Wako, 121-05063). The proteins were digested overnight. The peptide solution was separated from the beads by centrifugation at 1’500x g for 20 min, washed with 100 µL µL NH4CO3 (100 mM) and centrifuged again 1’500x g for 40 min. The samples were acidified with formic acid to a final concentration of 5%. The C18 plate (HNFR S18V, Nest Group) was activated with ACN and equilibrated three times with 5% ACN, 0.1% FA. The peptides were then desalted using the C18-plate. The peptides were washed three times with 5% ACN, 0.1% FA. Elution of the peptides was performed with 2x 100 µL 40% ACN (0.1% FA). The peptides were dried in a speed vacuum centrifuge. The samples were resuspended in 20 µL 2% ACN, 0.1% FA containing 0.2x iRT peptides (Biognosys). The data was acquired in data independent acquisition mode. Pooled condition samples were used to acquire library data in data dependent acquisition mode.

DATA PROCESSING:
Acquired MS/MS spectra were searched against the UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot protein database (downloaded on 17.04.2019; http:/www.uniprot.org/) using the Spectronaut software (v 18.6.231227.55695). Trypsin/P and LysC/P were selected as the digestion enzymes. The search parameters were set to allow only fully tryptic peptides with maximum four missed cleavages. Carbamidomethyl (C) was set as fixed peptide modification and Biotin (K), Phosphorylation (S, T, Y), Acetyl (protein N-term) and Oxidation (M) as variable modifications. The analysis type was peptide-centric. The precursor Qvalue Cutoff was 0.01. The precursor filtering was set to “Identified (Qvalue)”. No imputation or Cross-Run Normalization was done in the software.
