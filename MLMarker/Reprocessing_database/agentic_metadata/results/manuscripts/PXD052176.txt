ABSTRACT:
Proteomic glycosylation mapping via mass spectrometry and co-immunoprecipitation assays elucidates the MGAT4A interaction with EGFR, catalyzing N-glycosylation at the EGFR N604 site.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Targeting MGAT4A-mediated N-glycosylation as a therapeutic strategy to inhibit glioblastoma stem cell invasion

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Protein extraction and sample preparation Collect 83 GSC infected with MGAT4A-overexpression and vector-control lentivirus for protein extraction. Added 4 times the volume of lysis buffer (8M urea; 1% protease inhibitor) to each sample followed by ultrasonic lysis and centrifugation. The supernatant was transferred to a new centrifuge tube, and the protein concentration was measured with a BCA kit (Vazyme). An equal amount of each sample was taken for enzymatic hydrolysis, and the volume was adjusted to the same with the lysis solution. Slowly add the final concentration of 20% TCA, vortex mix well, and precipitate at 4 ℃ for 2 hours. The precipitate was collected after centrifuging at 4,500 g for 5 min and washing twice with pre-cooled acetone. TEAB was added to the pellet to a final concentration of 200 mM after drying and then ultrasonically dispersed. Trypsin was added at a ratio of 1:50 (protease: protein, m/m), and hydrolysed overnight. Dithiothreitol (DTT) was added to a final concentration of 5 mM and incubated at 56°C for 30 min. Next, iodoacetamide (IAA) was added to make a final concentration of 11 mM and incubated for 15 min at room temperature in the dark. Dissolve the peptide in IP buffer solution (100 mM NaCl; 1 mM EDTA; 50 mM Tris-HCl; 0.5% NP-40; pH 8.0), transfer the supernatant to the N-glycosylated resin that has been washed (antibody resin item number 23263910488716510, from Hangzhou Jingjie Biotechnology Co., Ltd., PTM Bio), placed on a 4 ℃ rotating shaker, gently shake and incubate overnight. After the incubation, the resin was washed 4 times with IP buffer solution and twice with deionized water. Finally, use 0.1% trifluoroacetic acid eluent to elute the resin-bound peptide, eluting three times, collecting the eluent and vacuum freeze and drain. After draining, follow the instructions of C18 ZipTips for desalination, vacuum freeze draining, and then use for LC/MS analysis.

DATA PROCESSING:
LC-MS/MS analysis  The peptides were dissolved in liquid chromatography mobile phase A and separated using the EASY-nLC 1000 UPLC (Ultra Performance Liquid Chromatography) system. The mobile phase A was an aqueous solution containing 0.1% formic acid and 2% acetonitrile, and the mobile phase B contained 0.1% formic acid and 90% acetonitrile. The liquid phase gradient was set as follows: 0–40 min, 5–20% B; 40–52 min, 20–32% B; 52–56 min, 30–80% B;56-60 min, 80% B, and maintain flow rate at 500 nl/min. The peptides were analyzed with an NSI source through tandem mass spectrometry (MS/MS) in Q Exactive TM plus and coupled with UPLC online. The electrospray voltage was 2.2 kV, and the peptides are detected and analyzed using high-resolution Orbitrap. The scanning range of the primary mass spectrometry is 350-1800 m/z at a resolution of 70000; the scanning range of the secondary mass spectrometry is fixed at a starting point of 100 m/z, resolution of 17500. The data acquisition mode used the data dependency scanning (DDA) program, that was, after the first level scanning, the parent ion of the first 10 peptide segment with the highest signal strength was selected to enter the HCD collision pool in turn to use 28% of the fragmentation energy for fragmentation, and the secondary mass spectrometry analysis was also carried out in turn. In order to improve the effective utilization of mass spectrometry, the automatic gain control (AGC) was set to 5E4, the signal threshold was set to 5E3 ions/s. The maximum injection time was set to 200 ms, and the dynamic exclusion time of tandem mass spectrometry scanning was set to 15 s to avoid repeated scanning of the parent ions.
