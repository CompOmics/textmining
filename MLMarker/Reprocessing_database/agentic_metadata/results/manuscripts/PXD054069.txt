ABSTRACT:
Introduction: Angong Niuhuang Wan (AGNHW), developed in the Qing dynasty (18th century) for treating consciousness disturbances caused by severe infections, has already been used to treat brain edema caused by ischemia-reperfusion. However, it remains unclear whether AGNHW can ameliorate vascular-origin brain edema caused by lipopolysaccharides (LPS).This study explores the ameliorative effects of AGNHW on cerebrovascular edema in mice caused by LPS, as well as its potential mechanisms.Mice were divided into four groups: Control, LPS 26 h, LPS 48 h, and LPS + AGNHW_M. Enriched phosphorylated peptides were extracted from mouse brain tissues at the corresponding time points and subjected to phosphoproteomic analysis.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
LC/MS Analysis of Phosphorylated Protein Changes in Mouse Brain Tissue After LPS Treatment and Angong Niuhuang Wan Administration

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Mix immobilized metal affinity chromatography (IMAC) material with titanium sulfate, dissolve in ultrapure water, and sonicate at 40 kHz. Vortex to mix and stir at room temperature with a magnetic stirrer for 12 hours. Centrifuge (15000 g, 5 minutes) and discard the supernatant. Disperse the material in 0.1% trifluoroacetic acid (TFA) aqueous solution. Wash by shaking and then centrifuge (15000 g, 5 minutes), discard the supernatant, and repeat the washing 5 times to remove unchelated Ti4+. Wash the material twice with a sodium chloride/0.1% TFA aqueous solution and then twice with 0.1% TFA. Resuspend the prepared material in 0.1% TFA and store at 4°C.  Preconfigure LB extraction buffer containing protease inhibitors and phosphatase inhibitors. Add 500 μl of LB extraction buffer per 50 mg of brain tissue and homogenize on ice using a sonicator (AMP 50%). Quantify the proteins using the Bradford method, add dithiothreitol to a final concentration of 10 mmol/L, and incubate at 37°C for 1 hour. Add iodoacetamide to a final concentration of 50 mmol/L, incubate in the dark at 37°C for 1 hour, and then centrifuge at 20000 g for 15 minutes.  Perform ultrafiltration using a 4 ml, 10 kDa ultrafiltration tube. Add the sample to the ultrafiltration tube and fill up to 4 ml with 50 mmol/L ammonium bicarbonate. Centrifuge at 4°C (3500 g, 20 minutes) until the upper phase is reduced to 500 μl, and repeat once. Refill the sample to 1 ml with 50 mmol/L ammonium bicarbonate, add trypsin, and incubate at 37°C for 12 hours. Add 0.1% formic acid to terminate the reaction.  Add 80% acetonitrile (ACN)/6% TFA and Ti4+-IMAC material to the protein digest. Shake at room temperature for 30 minutes, then centrifuge (15000 g, 5 minutes) and discard the supernatant. Resuspend the material in 50% ACN/6% TFA/200 mmol/L sodium chloride aqueous solution, shake for 30 minutes, then centrifuge (15000 g, 5 minutes) and discard the supernatant. Resuspend the material in 30% ACN/0.1% TFA aqueous solution, shake for 30 minutes, then centrifuge (15000 g, 5 minutes) and discard the supernatant. Add 10% ammonia solution, sonicate on ice for 5 minutes, shake for 15 minutes, then centrifuge (20000 g, 5 minutes) and collect the supernatant. Centrifuge the supernatant (20000 g, 5 minutes) and evaporate using a rotary evaporator. Redissolve in 0.1% formic acid. The peptide sample was loaded onto an LC-MS system with three replicates. The nano scale liquid chromatography system EASY-nLC 1000 (Thermo Fisher Scientiﬁc) was coupled with a mass spectrometer Q-Exactive (Thermo Fisher Scientiﬁc) via the nano electrospray source. Reverse-phase chromatography and a trap column packed with 2 cm long, 100 μm inner diameter fused silica trap column containing 5.0 μm Aqua C18 beads (Thermo Fisher Scientiﬁc) were used for peptide enrichment. The peptides were loaded onto the trap column at a ﬂow rate of 5 μL/min, and then eluted from the analytical column (15cm long, 75μm inner diameter fused silica column filing with 3.0 μm Aqua C18 beads, Thermo Fisher Scientific). Peptides were gradient eluted at a flow rate of 350 nL/min with the following conditions: 100% buffer A (0.1% formic acid) to 8% buffer B (0.1% formic acid, 80% acetonitrile) for 10 min, 8% to 20% buffer B for 80 min, 20 to 30% buffer B for 20 min, 30% to 90% buffer B for 5 min, then 90% buffer B for 10 min. The eluted peptides from the analytical column were directly injected into the mass spectrometer via nano-ESI source. Ion signals were collected in a data-dependent mode with the following settings:  full scan resolution at 70,000, scan range: m/z 300-1,800; precursor ions were fragmented by high energy collision-induced dissociation mode with MS/MS scan resolution of 17,500, isolation window 2 m/z, normalized collision energy of 27, loop count 20. Dynamic exclusion was also used (charge exclusion: unassigned 1 >8; peptide match: preferred; exclude isotopes: on; dynamic exclusion: 10 s). The MS/MS data was collected and saved in raw files through the Xcalibur software (version 2.2, Thermo Fisher Scientiﬁc).
