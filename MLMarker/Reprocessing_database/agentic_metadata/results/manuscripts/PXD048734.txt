PRIDE PROJECT PROPERTIES:
organisms: Homo sapiens (human)
instruments: Orbitrap Fusion Lumos
modifications: No PTMs are included in the dataset
experiment types: Data-independent acquisition; Data-dependent acquisition
keywords: Lc-ms

ABSTRACT:
Protein identification in 58 cancer tissues, including breast, kidney, ovary, colon liver, stomach,  esophagus, lung

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Proteome of 58 pancancer identified by LC-MS

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
The proteins isolated from FFPE tissue were dissolved in 4% SDS lysis buffer and tryptic digested through filter alded sample preparation (FASP) method.

DATA PROCESSING:
We use the ProteomeDiscoverer (Thermo Scientific) and Spectronaut (Biognosys) software to search the  library and analysis.
