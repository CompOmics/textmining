ABSTRACT:
Using proteome integral solubility alteration (PISA) to assess HDAC inhibitor targets across ovarian cancer and acute lymphoblastic leukemia cell types

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Targets of histone deacetylase inhibitors in ovarian cancer identified using proteome integral solubility alteration

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
adapted sample processing protocol from the PISA bioprotocols original paper. briefly: cells were treated with HDACi briefly, then subjected to a temperature gradient, lysed and then subject to standard proteomic processing with TMT labeling

DATA PROCESSING:
MaxQuant was used to search the files, TMT quantification was added into the search parameters. Results were processed in perseus with contaminants removed, normalized, and restricted to quantitative values only
