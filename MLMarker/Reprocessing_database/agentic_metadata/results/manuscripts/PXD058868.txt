ABSTRACT:
N-terminome analysis of HeLa proteome under the stress of unfolded protein response. UPR was derived by combinational treatment of MG132 and thapsigargin. N-terminal enrichment was performed by iNrich method (10.1021/acs.analchem.9b05653). Data was prepared to have duplicates.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
N-terminome analysis of HeLa cells with UPR stress

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
HeLa was grown in DMEM (Gibco, Rockville, MD, USA) medium supplemented with 10% FBS (Gibco) and 1% penicillin/streptomycin (Gibco). Cultures were maintained in an atmosphere of 5% CO2 and 95% air in a humidified incubator at 37C. Cells were grown to >90% confluence. For ER stress experiments, HCT116 cells were treated with 5 µg/mL tunicamycin (TM) (Sigma-Aldrich) or 500 nM thapsigargin (TG) (Sigma-Aldrich). Cells were harvested by trypsinization, washed twice with ice-cold PBS (phosphate-buffered saline, pH 7.4; Gibco), and resuspended in ice-cold lysis buffer containing 0.2 M EPPS (pH 8.0), 6 M guanidine, 10 mM TCEP (Thermo Fisher Scientific), and 40 mM 2-chloroacetamide (Sigma-Aldrich). After 10 min incubation on 95 °C, cells were lysed by ultrasonication by BranSonic 400B. The proteins from the cell lysate were isolated by transferring supernatant after centrifugation at 12,000 g for 10 min at 4C. The protein concentration of the collected supernatant was determined by BCA (bicinchoninic acid) protein assay.  Precipitated HeLa samples (1 mg proteins) were reconstituted in 0.2 mL reaction buffer (6 M guanidine in 0.2 M EPPS, pH 8.0), measured protein concentration with BCA protein quantification kit (Thermo), diluted further to 4 mg/mL. Proteins in samples were labeled with 200 mM D6-acetic anhydride and 200 mM pyridine for 2 hours at 25C with end-over-end rotation. Labeled proteins in samples were digested by adding trypsin or chymotrypsin (Promega, 1:50 enzyme-to-substrate ratio) and incubating overnight at 25C with end-over-end rotation. Depletion of internal peptides was performed by adding 330 mg of NHS-activated agarose dry resin and incubating for 2 hours at 25C with end-over-end rotation. N-terminal peptides were washed for 20 mL of 0.1% trifluoroacetic acid (TFA), were eluted using 1 mL of 0.1% formic acid (FA) in 50% acetonitrile (ACN). N-terminal peptides were dried by vacuum centrifugation and stored at -20C until peptide fractionation.  For basic reversed phase liquid chromatography (bRPLC) of N-terminal peptide samples, 100 μg peptides were reconstituted in bRPLC solvent A (10 mM ammonium formate, pH 10) and loaded onto a XBridge BEH C18 RPLC column, 130Å, 3.5 μm (4.6 * 250 mm) and coupled to a 1290 UHPLC system (Agilent). Samples were washed using bRPLC solvent A for 10 min at 0.5 mL/min and subsequently eluted applying a two-step gradient from 0 to 40% bRPLC solvent B (10 mM ammonium formate, pH 10 in 90% ACN) in 38.5 min, to 70% in 14 min, and holding at 70% for 10 min. 168 fractions (0.5 min each) were collected and pooled and the resulting 12 fractions were dried down and stored at  -20C until LC-MS analysis.  LC-MS measurements of N-terminal peptide samples were performed with an Ultimate 3000 RSLCnano system coupled to a Q-Exactive mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific). bRPLC fractionated peptide samples were reconstituted in 5 μL of 0.1% FA in 2% ACN. 2 µL of samples were injected onto a PepMap 100 trap column (75 µm  20 mm, Thermo Fisher Scientific), washed with 0.1% FA in 2% ACN for 10 min at a flow rate of 5 µL/min and subsequently transferred to an EASY-Spray PepMap RSLC, 2 µm analytical column (75 µm  500 mm, Thermo Fisher Scientific). Peptides were separated at 300 nL/min using a 110 min linear gradient from 2.5 to 37.5% LC solvent B (0.1% FA in 80% ACN) in LC solvent A (0.1% FA). MS1 spectra were recorded in the Orbitrap from 400 to 1800 m/z at a resolution of 70,000 and using an automatic gain control (AGC) target value of 1e6 charges and a maximum injection time (maxIT) of 30 ms. Up to 12 of the most abundant precursors (topN) were selected for HCD fragmentation at 27% normalized collision energy (NCE). MS2 spectra were acquired at 17,500 resolution using an isolation window of 2.0 m/z, an AGC target value of 5e4 charges, and a maxIT of 120 ms. The dynamic exclusion was set to 30 s..

DATA PROCESSING:
Constructing Nt-arginylation specific decoy database for FDR estimation of MS2 prediction module. A UniProt human reference protein database (Release 2023_02) was used to construct decoy databases. For a trypsin decoy database, any consecutive arginine residues more than 2 in original protein database were replaced to a single arginine. For a chymotrypsin database, any arginine after consecutive combination of phenylalanine, leucine, methionine, tryptophan, and tyrosine more than 2 in original database was removed.  Tandem database searching. Proteome Discoverer v2.4 with its built-in search engine SequestHT was used to identify and quantify peptides for N-terminomics experiments. Fragment mass spectra were searched against the UniProt human protein database supplemented with common contaminants. Unless stated otherwise, Proteome Discoverer’s default parameters were applied. These included for all searches: carbamidomethylation of cysteine and D3acetylation of lysine as fixed modification, oxidation of methionine; cleavage specificity was set to amino terminal specific; precursor tolerance was set to ±10 ppm and fragment ion tolerance to ±0.05 Da. Max. missed cleavage sites was set to 2 for trypsin dataset, and 5 for chymotrypsin dataset. For Parameters included for the first search part of tandem database searching: N-terminal acetylation and N-terminal D3acetylation as variable modification. The second search utilized spectra that Percolator did not validate as input data. Parameters included for the second search part of tandem database searching: pyro-glutamation at N-terminal glutamate, D3-acetyl-arginylation (+201.1305 Da) at N-terminal aspartate and glutamate, and D3-acetyl-arginylation-deamidation (+202.1145 Da) at N-terminal asparagine and glutamine as variable modification. Minora feature detector module was used to perform an LFQ. Parameters for consensus workflow regarding to LFQ: label Precursor abundance was based on area and internal normalization for experimental bias correction was set to total peptide amount.  Database searching for gathering arg-starting peptides and decoy Nt-arginylation peptides. For the training dataset containing arg-starting peptides for training MS2 prediction module and a dataset containing decoy Nt-arginylation peptides for estimating Nt-arginylation specific FDR, database searching was performed only once. Same parameters were applied except these: cleavage specificity was set to specific at both ends; N-terminal acetylation, N-terminal D3-acetylation, and pyro-glutamation at N-terminal glutamate as variable modification. The Nt-arginylation specific decoy databases were used to identify decoy Nt-arginylation peptides dataset.
