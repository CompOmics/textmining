PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
LDLR and HMGCR double knock out human HepG2 cells LC-MSMS

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
Cells were washed twice with pre-chilled PBS, and dissolved in 500 uL buffer (6M Urea, 0.1M NaHCO3, 0.2% SDS) plus protease inhibitor. After 5 min of ultrasonication on ice, the lysate was centrifuged and the extracts were reduced with 10 mM DTT at 56 °C for 1 h. Then the samples were alkylated with iodoacetamide at room temperature for 1 h, and washed twice with acetone, the pellet was dissolved by 0.1 M triethylammonium bicarbonate (TEAB) and 6 M urea. 120 µg of protein sample was digested by trypsin and TEAB at 37 °C for 16 h. 1% formic acid was added. After centrifugation, the supernatant was loaded to the C18 desalting column at a lower speed. The eluents were collected and lyophilized. The samples were mixed with 0.1 M TEAB and acetonitrile-dissolved TMT labeling reagent. Equal volume of all labelling samples was mixed. The sample was fractionated by mobile phase A (2% acetonitrile, adjusted pH to 10.0 using ammonium hydroxide) and B (98% acetonitrile, adjusted pH to 10.0 using ammonium hydroxide) using C18 column.
