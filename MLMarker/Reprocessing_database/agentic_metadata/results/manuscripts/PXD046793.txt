ABSTRACT:
Immortalized mouse macrophages (IMMs) were stimulated with lipopolysaccharide (LPS).  RNA and RNA associated proteins were UV crosslinked, and sites of RNA-association were identified using LC-MS proteomics.

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
LPS-Stimulated Mouse Macrophage RNA Binding Protein Domain Enrichment and Analysis

SAMPLE PROCESSING:
IMMs were LPS stimulated for 0, 30, and 60 min.  UV crosslinking of RNA to RNA-associated proteins was performed.  The cells were lysed by adding a lysis buffer and bead beating was performed.  Silica columns were used to bind RNA and enrich the RNA-crosslinked proteins.  The proteins were digested using trypsin + Lys-C.  Nuclease P1 was used to digest the RNA.  The samples were LC-MS analyzed on a Thermo UltiMate 3000 - Orbitrap Eclipse.

DATA PROCESSING:
Proteome Discoverer version 3.0.0.757 was used to analyze the LC-MS data.  NuXL was included as a Proteome Discoverer plugin and was used to identify peptides crosslinked to digested RNA.  The spectra were searched against a mouse proteome database downloaded from UniProt.
