## 1. Executive Summary

This report analyzes the text-mined metadata corpus generated by HAMLET from public proteomics repositories (PRIDE). The primary objective is to decompose the normalized metadata dataset (`HAMLET_normalized.tsv`), isolate **human primary tissue samples**, exclude non-tissue experimental models (in vitro cell cultures, cell lines, organoids, xenografts), assess compatibility with **MLMarker**'s proteomic tissue prediction model, and identify opportunities to augment the official MLMarker training corpus (`SupplementaryTableS1.tsv`).

- **Total Raw Entries:** 1,737 public proteomics projects (PXDs).
- **Human Primary Cohort Retained:** **424 projects (24.4%)** after multi-step exclusion.
- **Known Tissue Annotations:** **331 projects (78.1% of retained)** have an explicit anatomical tissue origin.
- **MLMarker Overall Compatibility:** **134 projects (31.6% of retained; 40.5% of known tissues)** align directly with MLMarker's 34-tissue reference repertoire.
- **Dominant Health Context:** **208 projects (49.1%)** represent diseased/pathological states, **95 (22.4%)** represent healthy/normal baselines, **45 (10.6%)** are mixed case-control studies, and **76 (17.9%)** lack health status annotations.
- **Training Dataset Overlap:** Out of the 183 training PXDs in `SupplementaryTableS1.tsv`, **86 (47.0%)** are in the retained human primary cohort, **53 (29.0%)** are in the excluded in vitro/cell-line cohort, and **44 (24.0%)** are outside HAMLET.
- **New Candidate Datasets for Training Expansion:** HAMLET provides **338 retained human primary tissue PXDs** that are NOT currently in `SupplementaryTableS1.tsv`, including **24 high-priority Healthy/Normal and Case-Control Mixed datasets** across under-represented target tissues (Liver, Bone Marrow, Placenta, Heart, Skeletal Muscle, Stomach, Brain, Lung, and Kidney).

## 2. Sample Filtering & Exclusion Methodology

High-throughput proteomics archives frequently combine patient biopsies with cell culture models, engineered cell lines, and animal xenografts. To ensure that downstream comparisons reflect physiological human tissue proteomes, samples were filtered through the following criteria:

1. **Species Restriction:** Retain only projects where `species` corresponds strictly to `Homo sapiens` (excluding non-human models such as *Mus musculus*, *S. cerevisiae*, and multi-species benchmark mixtures).
2. **Explicit Exclusion of In Vitro & Non-Primary Systems:**
   - **Cell Cultures & Cell Lines:** Exclude all samples where `sample_source` or `material_type` mentions `'cell culture'`, `'cell line'`, or specific cultured cell phenotypes (e.g. `'primary B-CLL cells'`, `'primary hepatocytes'`, in vitro model systems).
   - **Organoids:** Exclude 3D in vitro culture models (`'organoid'`).
   - **Xenografts & PDX:** Exclude patient-derived xenografts (`'xenograft'`, `'pdx'`).
   - **Synthetic/Recombinant:** Exclude recombinant proteins, synthetic peptide ladders, and calibration standards.

## 3. Core Stratification & 2×2 Cross-Tabulation Matrix

The 424 retained samples were stratified across two primary axes:
1. **Health Status:**
   - **Healthy / Normal:** Samples documented with `'normal'` or `'disease free'` without any co-occurring disease terms.
   - **Diseased / Tumor / Pathological:** Samples documented with confirmed pathological terms (e.g., carcinoma, fibrosis, infection, inflammation).
   - **Mixed (Case-Control):** Studies intentionally designed with both diseased tissue and matched adjacent normal / healthy controls.
   - **Unknown:** Samples where disease status was recorded as `'unknown'` or omitted.
2. **MLMarker Compatibility:**
   - **Compatible:** Tissue term matches one of MLMarker's 34 target human tissue classes.
   - **Incompatible:** Tissue term is either not covered by MLMarker (e.g. biofluids like blood plasma, serum, urine; or uncovered organs like skin, breast, cervix, pancreas) or missing.

## 6. Key Takeaways & Quality Flags

1. **Liquid Biofluid Dominance (~120 projects):** Whole blood, plasma, serum, urine, and CSF make up a large fraction of proteomics datasets. MLMarker is trained on cellular proteomes (identifying `Monocytes` / `B-cells` rather than fluid matrices).
2. **Missing Solid Organs in MLMarker:** Important clinical tissues such as **Skin** (12), **Breast** (10), **Cervix** (6), and **Pancreas** (4) are currently omitted from MLMarker's 34 target classes.
3. **Rescue Opportunity for Unknown Tissues (93 projects, 21.9%):** 93 retained projects completely lack tissue annotations in PRIDE metadata. MLMarker provides high utility here by performing expression-based tissue rescue directly on the raw protein quantification matrices.
4. **Project-Level vs. Run-Level Granularity:** 45 case-control studies combine healthy controls and tumor tissue under a single PXD accession. While HAMLET records a composite label at the project level, MLMarker decomposes these into individual run-level predictions.
